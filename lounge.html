Panna Lounge & Bar

Unwind at Rohtak's premium rooftop lounge featuring handcrafted mocktails, vibrant music, and city skyline views.

Signature Rooftop Cocktails

Our expert bartenders serve a curated list of refreshing mocktails, cocktails, and drinks to pair with custom finger foods. Ask for a classic done perfectly or let the bar surprise you with a Page signature built around seasonal flavours. Every pour comes with skyline views and unhurried service.

Unwind in comfortable sofas under string lighting, accompanied by soft lounge tunes and refreshing night air. Weekends bring curated playlists and a gentle buzz that lasts late into the evening. It is the rare kind of nightlife — lively enough to celebrate, calm enough to talk.
